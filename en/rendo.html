Brand

From a last in Asakusa.

RENDO is the shoe line of pattern maker Teppei Yoshimi. The lasts are carved in the Asakusa atelier from measurements of Japanese feet. The toe stays quiet.

Most of the shoes are Goodyear welted, so the sole can be repaired. This page is the eight ready-made and made-to-order styles on the official store.

A pattern order, cut from your own last, is a different commission. It starts at ¥105,600, tax included. See what that includes
