SHOES

From heels to soles and polishing. Some prices differ for women’s and men’s shoes.

  • Heel
    ladies ¥1,650〜
    men's ¥3,520〜

    The worn rubber on the heel is replaced. If the heel is wearing down, an early change helps.

  • Half sole
    ladies ¥3,300〜
    men's ¥3,850〜

    Rubber is added to reinforce the sole. Useful when the sole is wearing, or when the shoe feels slippery.

  • Full sole
    ladies ¥9,900〜
    men's ¥18,700〜

    The sole is replaced with a new one.

  • Polish
    ladies ¥1,100〜
    men's ¥1,650〜

    Dirt is cleaned off, then cream feeds the leather and brings back a shine. Regular care helps.

  • Cleaning ¥3,300〜

    The shoes are washed, old cream and dirt are removed, and ozone treatment cleans through to the inside. For sweat or odor.

BAG

Fraying, zippers, handles, and other parts that wear with use. Piping, hardware, and leather jackets are quoted after we see them.

  • Split seam ¥1,650〜

    Loose thread and broken stitching are repaired. Left alone, a small break can spread.

  • Zipper ¥5,500〜

    A broken or stiff zipper is replaced. Many zipper types can be handled.

  • Handle ¥19,800〜

    A worn handle is replaced, kept as close as possible to the original material and shape.
