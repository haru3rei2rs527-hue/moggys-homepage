About

A pair that is not in the ready-made line

A pattern order is a custom shoe made in a design, leather, or last that is not in the ready-made line.

  • A design that is not ready-made

  • A specification of your own

  • A ready-made pair that does not quite fit

Once a year, Nagoya holds the order consultation at this studio. Shops that take orders year-round, and online help when a visit is difficult, are listed in RENDO’s official note.

Flow

How an order proceeds

  1. 01

    Measure the feet

    The feet are measured at the shop.

  2. 02

    Choose the last and size

    From the measurements, size and last are chosen after a fitting sample.

  3. 03

    Choose the design and materials

    Design, upper, lining, and sole. Line changes and other options are decided here.

  4. 04

    Make the order sheet

    The specification is written on a sheet, and that is the order.
