Baby Shoes

Shoes for enjoying
a child’s growing years, together.

Leather baby shoes for the first years of walking.

One size, 13 cm, for a child of about one and a half to two years, when walking outdoors begins.

Laces and side elastic make the shoes easy to put on, and still hold the foot.

A few words can be stamped inside the tongue and on the wooden box. A name or a date can stay with the shoes as a record of that time.

After the child outgrows them, the shoes can stay with the wooden box as something to keep in the room.

Looking back at the shoes from those small years,
Someday, looking at them, you might say how small those days were.

The shoes were made so that time can stay with them.

How to order

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    We ask about the leather color, the design, and the words to stamp. Please look at the sample and talk through the finished pair.

  2. 02

    Payment

    Please confirm the specification and the price, then pay. Making starts after payment is confirmed.

  3. 03

    Making

    Each pair is made after the order. Finishing takes about one to one and a half months.

  4. 04

    Ready to collect

    We contact you when the pair is ready. It can be collected at the studio or sent.
