About

ABOUT

A studio in Takami, Chikusa.
Shoes and bags are repaired here, and leather shoes are sold here too.

Ready-made,
toward bespoke.

A ready-made shoe is not yet that person’s shoe when it is new. With wear and repair it follows the foot, settles into the walk, and grows into a pair that belongs to a life.

MOGGYS SHOE STUDIO aims at repair that turns ready-made shoes toward bespoke. Not only a new sole or a rebuilt heel, but a pair you can keep. That is the work.

Sneakers, leather shoes, and bags.
New and used leather shoes are also here. Drop-off at the studio, and post from anywhere in Japan.

Owner

A note from Hara

“If only this shoe were a little more like this.”

Giving that wish a shape.

That is what shoe repair means at MOGGYS SHOE STUDIO.

A ready-made shoe is made for many people.

Once you wear it,
“a little more like this here,”
“this part bothers me,”
those thoughts come up.

To settle the fit.
To change the design toward yourself.
To keep caring for it until it is a pair you can stay with.

Little by little, it becomes that person’s shoe.

“Ready-made, toward bespoke.”

From that idea, each pair is met with repair, leather care, or a change.

Not only to fix it,
but so you like the shoe more.

“If only this shoe were a little more like this.”

The work is to answer that feeling.

Hara / MOGGYS SHOE STUDIO
